Types of Teleprompter Setups for Corporate Town Halls

Presidential (Glass) Teleprompters

The classic setup with two glass panels flanking the speaker. Text reflects from monitors below while remaining transparent to the audience. This is the ideal choice for:

  • Large audience town halls at venues like the Greater Columbus Convention Center, Huntington Convention Center of Cleveland, or Duke Energy Convention Center in Cincinnati
  • Keynote presentations at company-wide meetings
  • Any event where the speaker addresses a live audience while maintaining a professional stage presence
  • Multi-speaker panels and executive roundtables

Camera-Mounted Teleprompters

For town halls that are being filmed or streamed live, camera-mounted prompters allow speakers to read while maintaining direct eye contact with the lens. This setup is perfect for:

  • Company-wide virtual meetings broadcast to remote employees
  • Investor presentations and earnings calls
  • Internal video messages recorded for company intranets
  • Press conferences and media briefings

Confidence Monitors

Floor-level monitors that allow speakers to glance at their talking points without the audience noticing. These work well for:

  • Q&A sessions where speakers need reminders of key talking points
  • Moderated panels and discussion formats
  • Backup support for experienced speakers who prefer minimal prompting
  • Smaller executive meetings in hotel boardrooms

The Operator Makes the Difference

We've all seen the town hall that goes wrong—the CEO who loses their place, the awkward pauses, the moment when someone clearly forgets what comes next. A professional teleprompter operator prevents those moments by matching scroll speed to the speaker's natural rhythm, anticipating pace changes, and making real-time adjustments throughout the presentation.

Here's what a professional teleprompter operator brings to your corporate town hall:

  • Rhythm matching: Adjusting scroll speed to match each speaker's natural speaking pace
  • Anticipation: Preparing for expected pauses, questions, and ad-lib moments
  • Real-time corrections: Implementing last-minute script changes without missing a beat
  • Talent comfort: Keeping speakers calm and confident throughout the presentation
  • Director coordination: Communicating seamlessly with AV teams and production staff

Planning Your Corporate Town Hall Teleprompter

Key Questions to Consider

  • How many speakers? Multi-speaker events require more setup time and coordination
  • Live or recorded? This determines whether you need camera-mounted or presidential-style prompters
  • Audience size: Large audiences at venues like Nationwide Arena may need larger monitors or multiple prompter stations
  • Virtual component? If employees are watching remotely, camera-mounted prompters ensure natural eye contact
  • Script timing: How long should the presentation run? Operators can adjust pacing to hit timing targets

Timing Recommendations

For major corporate town halls at Ohio venues, we recommend:

  • Book 3-4 weeks in advance for large venue events
  • Schedule a 30-60 minute rehearsal the day of or day before
  • Have a final script locked 48 hours before the event
  • Build in 15-20 minutes for technical setup and sound check

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book teleprompter services for a corporate town hall?

For major events at large venues like the Greater Columbus Convention Center or Huntington Convention Center, we recommend booking 3-4 weeks in advance. For smaller corporate events, a week or two is typically sufficient. That said, we've accommodated last-minute requests when our schedule allows.

Can executives rehearse with the teleprompter before the event?

Absolutely. We always recommend a rehearsal where executives can get comfortable with the equipment, try different scroll speeds, and identify any script issues. This is included in our standard service for corporate events.

What happens if we need to change the script last minute?

It happens regularly—executive communications teams revise remarks right up to show time. Our software allows real-time edits, and our operators are experienced at implementing changes on the fly without disrupting the flow of the presentation.

Do you provide teleprompters for virtual town halls?

Yes. We regularly support virtual and hybrid town halls for Ohio companies. Camera-mounted teleprompters are ideal for these events, as they allow speakers to maintain direct eye contact with the camera while reading their talking points.

Can you work with our existing AV company?

Yes. We frequently coordinate with venue AV teams and production companies throughout Ohio. We're experienced at integrating with existing setups and will coordinate with your AV provider to ensure seamless execution.
